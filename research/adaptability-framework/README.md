# AI-Era Organizational Adaptability

> **Scope:** How AI usage, organizational structure, and technical infrastructure change together as AI capabilities evolve.
> **Status:** Research draft. Plan and eleven memos compiled on 2026-04-22. Source audit and final synthesis pending.

This track extends the repository's [AI Systems Design & Management frame](../ai-systems/README.md). It asks how organizations keep AI-based decision systems effective through repeated model, workflow, and infrastructure changes.

Start with the [research plan](plan.md) for the two anchor questions, three pillars, and research prompts. Read M1 for the proposed system model and M2 for its measurement design.

## Evidence and next steps
