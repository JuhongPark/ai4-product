# AI in Revenue Operations & Sales Enablement

> **Date:** 2026-04-21
> **Scope:** Peer-reviewed and practitioner research on B2B sales AI (2024–2026)

---

## 1. Summary

Revenue Operations (RevOps) is the unification of sales, marketing, and customer success under shared data, systems, and KPIs. AI in 2026 is both a *cause* and *effect* of this unification: AI tools require unified data to work, and their outputs force further consolidation of formerly siloed workflows.

## 4. RevOps as Architectural Prerequisite

The 2026 B2B trend is that old silos (SDR queue, AE book, CS retention) break down because AI agents:

- Need unified customer records to personalize outreach
- Cross-reference product-usage telemetry (previously CS-owned) for upsell triggers
- Write marketing copy that must match sales-stage context

This creates a two-way causality: AI adoption forces RevOps; RevOps enables AI adoption.

---

## 5. Sales Enablement: From Content Library to Agentic Tutor

Historically sales enablement meant content libraries (decks, battlecards). In 2026:

- **AI generates decks/proposals on demand** per account per stage
- **Deal-level coaching** — AI reviews recorded calls, gives rep-specific feedback
- **Objection simulator** — LLM-based roleplay training at scale

Most production tools remain **assisted** (mode 2 of agentic framework) rather than autonomous.

---

## 6. Research Gaps

1. **Academic-industry gap** — Most RevOps AI literature is practitioner; peer-reviewed measurement scarce
2. **Cross-function attribution** — When AI touches lead → MQL → SQL → CW, attributing revenue to any function is unresolved
3. **Sales manager training curriculum** — Vorobyeva et al. identifies literacy gap but no validated programs exist
4. **Ethics of predictive scoring** — Intent scores discriminating leads by firmographic bias risk GDPR challenge
