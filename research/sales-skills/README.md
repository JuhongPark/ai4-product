# AI for Sales Skills (판매 + 투자)

> Scope: Application of AI to **selling (sales)** and **investing/fundraising** activities — how AI transforms the revenue-generation and capital-raising skillset of a product organization.

This area is a reframe of "AI for Product" around the **commercial and financial** dimensions of a product's life, distinct from implementation. Sales Skills here includes:

- **A1. Selling (판매)** — GTM, lead generation, sales enablement, pricing, CRM/CLV, revenue operations
- **A2. Investing & Fundraising (투자)** — Pitch decks, investor matching, IR, VC deal sourcing & due diligence

This complementary pair with [productization-skills/](../productization-skills/) divides the product-building skillset into **"what gets built"** (productization) vs **"how it is monetized and capitalized"** (sales).

## Sub-topics

### A1. Selling
- **Agentic Sales & AI SDR** — Autonomous agents replacing or augmenting sales development reps
- **Revenue Operations & Sales Enablement** — AI-driven RevOps unifying sales, marketing, CS data
- **Dynamic Pricing** — RL/ML for real-time price optimization
- **Sales Forecasting & CLV** — BLSTM, XGBoost, Gamma-Gamma models for pipeline/customer value prediction
- **AI Chatbots for Lead Qualification** — 24/7 qualification, demo scheduling

### A2. Investing & Fundraising
- **AI Pitch Deck & Investor Matching** — Deck generators (Slidebean, PitchBob, PitchGrade, Evalyze) with 150k+ investor DBs
- **AI Investor Relations** — Agentic IR workflows, earnings call simulation, sentiment monitoring
- **VC Deal Sourcing & Due Diligence** — Signal-based sourcing, NLP-driven DD compression
- **AI Startup Valuation** — Multi-method valuation tools (revenue multiple, DCF-lite, comp transactions)

## Key Stats (2026)

### Selling
- **81% of sales teams** use AI in 2026, up from ~50% in 2024; **54% deploy AI agents** across the sales cycle
- **AI SDR market**: CAGR 29.5%, projected **$15B by 2030**; 22% of teams have fully replaced human SDRs with AI
- **Gartner 2026**: AI agents will **outnumber human sellers 10x by 2028**; <40% of sellers will report AI agents improved productivity
- AI improves **win rates up to 25%**, **forecast accuracy 20–40%**, shortens cycles up to 25% (some sources: 30%)
- **AI-based pricing**: revenue +2–5%, margins +5–10% (McKinsey)

### Investing
- **82% of VCs** use AI for deal sourcing; **92% use AI** for due diligence & investor matching (Affinity survey, ~300 dealmakers)
- AI-driven sourcing reviews **3–5x more qualified opportunities** vs. network-dependent traditional approaches
- AI compresses DD timelines **from weeks to days**
- **Seed-stage AI startups: +42% valuation** vs non-AI peers; AI secured **~50% of global VC funding in 2025** ($202.3B)
- AI pitch deck platforms offer matching against **150,000+ investor databases** (Slidebean, PitchBob)

## Research Gaps

1. **Peer-reviewed effectiveness studies** — Most sales-AI adoption data comes from vendor surveys; academic validation is sparse relative to claim size
2. **Full-funnel AI sales orchestration** — Multi-agent handoff across SDR → AE → CS under-studied
3. **AI-originated deal outcomes** — No public longitudinal studies comparing IRR of AI-sourced vs. human-sourced VC deals
4. **Ethics & bias in AI pricing** — Limited research on fairness in dynamic pricing targeting individual willingness-to-pay
5. **IR regulatory risk** — Automated earnings Q&A and shareholder communications lack clear compliance frameworks
6. **Replacement cautionary patterns** — Analog to Klarna (product-CX) reversal: when full AI sales fails and hybrid reasserts
